# Choosing a model

The model you choose 

The first step in setting up vector search is choosing a model. If you want to generate your embeddings outside of OpenSearch, you can checkout a comprehensive list of models on [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/models). If you want to generate your embeddings inside of OpenSearch, you can upload a model to your OpenSearch cluster, use one of the pretrained models provided by OpenSearch, or connect to an externally hosted model. For more information, see [Integrating ML models]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/ml-commons-plugin/integrating-ml-models/). When choosing a model it is important to understand your workload requirements and have a strong evaluation plan.
